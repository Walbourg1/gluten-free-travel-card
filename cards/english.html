Gluten-free restaurant card in English

English · free to read, print or use in the app

This is a gluten-free restaurant card written in English. Show it to restaurant staff to explain that you have celiac disease, which foods are safe for you, which must be avoided, and why shared pans and cutting boards are a problem. It is free to use and free to print.

GLUTEN-FREE DIET

Note to the kitchen – I eat gluten-free

I have coeliac disease, a chronic autoimmune condition. I must avoid gluten completely – without any exception. Even tiny traces of gluten from crumbs, flour dust, or shared cooking utensils can make me seriously ill.

I can eat

  • Rice
  • Corn / Maize
  • Potatoes
  • Millet
  • Buckwheat
  • Amaranth
  • Quinoa
  • Vegetables
  • Salad
  • Fruit
  • Plain meat
  • Plain fish
  • Eggs
  • Milk & cheese
  • Legumes*
  • Cassava / Manioc / Yuca
  • Starch from potato, rice or maize

I must avoid

  • Wheat & wheat flour
  • Rye
  • Barley
  • Spelt
  • Regular oats
  • Bread & rolls
  • Regular pasta
  • Breadcrumbs / battered food
  • Flour-thickened sauces
  • Soy sauce
  • Regular beer

Common symbols for "gluten-free"

  • Please use only clean pots, pans, and chopping boards that have not come into contact with gluten-containing ingredients. A separate workspace is ideal. Please also check all ready-made products, sauces, and seasonings – soy sauce and many ready-made sauces often contain gluten.

Thank you so much for your care and understanding. You are helping me enjoy a safe and comfortable meal. Please feel free to ask me if you have any questions!

* Legumes: please check for added gluten-containing ingredients.

How to use this card

Show the card on your phone or hand over a printed copy when you order. If the staff would rather hear it than read it, the app can speak the whole card aloud in English. The app also works without any internet connection, which is usually more reliable than restaurant wifi.
