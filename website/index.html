Gluten Free Travel Card

A free gluten-free restaurant card in 50 languages. Works offline. No ads, no account, no data collection.

Gluten Free Travel Card is a free mobile app that shows restaurant staff a prepared card in their own language, explaining which foods are safe for someone with celiac disease, which must be avoided, and why shared pans and cutting boards cause a problem. It works fully offline and can read the card aloud in the local language.

Why it exists

Explaining celiac disease abroad is hard. Menus rarely say enough, language gets in the way, and the risk is not just gluten as an ingredient but traces from a shared pan or a floured board. Printed translation cards help, but they get forgotten at the hotel and only cover the languages you packed. This app is the version that is always in your pocket, in every language, without a connection.

What it does

  • 50 languages — pick your destination and show the card
  • Fully offline — no internet needed at the restaurant
  • Read aloud — the card is spoken in the local language
  • PDF export — print the card before travelling
  • Clear safe and unsafe food lists plus cross-contamination guidance
  • Free and private — no ads, no account, no data collection

Frequently asked questions

Is Gluten Free Travel Card free?

Yes, completely free. There are no ads, no user account, no in-app purchases and no paid tiers. The developer has celiac disease and built the app for personal use, then released it publicly.

Does the app work without internet?

Yes. All cards are stored inside the app, so it works fully offline. No internet connection is needed in the restaurant, which also means it works while roaming or in airplane mode.

How many languages are included?

50 languages, ranging from widely spoken ones such as Spanish, Arabic and Mandarin to Amharic, Thai and Vietnamese. Every card is also readable for free on this website.

Can the card be read aloud?

Yes. The app can speak the card in the local language using the phone’s built-in text-to-speech engine. This helps when restaurant staff would rather listen than read.

Can I print the card instead of using the app?

Yes. Every language page on this website is printable, and the app can export any card as a PDF before a trip.

Does the app collect personal data?

No. There is no account, no tracking, no analytics and no advertising. Preferences such as favourite languages stay on the device and are never transmitted.

Not medical advice. These cards are a communication aid, not a substitute for medical guidance. Always speak to restaurant staff directly and consult a doctor or dietitian about your own diet.
